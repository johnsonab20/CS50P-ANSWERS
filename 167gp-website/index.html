About

Patient capital.
Permanent partnerships.

At 167 General Partners, we believe the most durable businesses are built slowly, deliberately, and with the right partners at the table. We invest in profitable small businesses where we can add meaningful operational and strategic value — without disrupting what already works.

We take a founder-first approach. That means long hold periods, alignment on values before alignment on valuation, and a genuine commitment to the people who built something worth owning.

Thesis

What we look for.

01

Proven unit economics

We back businesses that are already profitable — not stories about future potential. Positive cash flow is non-negotiable.

02

Niche market leadership

Dominant in a defensible niche beats a small slice of a large market. We look for businesses with real pricing power and loyal customers.

03

Founder-led operations

We prefer businesses where the founder is still deeply involved and wants a true operational partner — not just a check.

04

Underserved by traditional PE

We operate at deal sizes that large funds ignore. That's our edge — less competition, more relationship-driven processes.
